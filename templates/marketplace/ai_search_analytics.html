How to read this

  • Percentiles, not means, for time. Search time is right-skewed (cache misses, slow API calls), so p50 shows a typical search and p95 the slow tail.
  • Distributions and groups. Histograms show spread; stage and query-length charts compare groups, each labeled with its sample size.
  • Trends. Daily counts show change over time; read short ranges with care when n is small.
  • Cost. Groq reports exact tokens; Gemini embedding tokens are estimated at 4 characters per token. Prices are paid-tier list prices; the projection assumes 5 AI searches per user per day, as in A7, and scales linearly.
